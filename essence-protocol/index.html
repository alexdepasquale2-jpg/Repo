A turn-based daemon-merging RPG

Essence
Protocol

Every thing in this world is merged from four essences, Fire, Water, Earth and Air, and sixteen sub-essences that bind to them. The daemons, artificial minds made of essence, can shape those merges. Every possible outcome has already been decided and is waiting for someone to find it. Bind daemons, compose merges, and take the four Warden keys to the Core.

Choose your first daemon

Each one is a Seed-tier merge of two main essences. It will grow sub-essences as it levels up and eventually recompile into new forms.
