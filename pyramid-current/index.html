The pyramids were never tombs. They were power plants. Beneath the sand, six copper coils still wait to feed the Core. Put on the fur hat, wake the grid, and burn out the Pyre before the Wardens lock it all down.

  • Move — drag anywhere (or WASD). You fire automatically.
  • Wake coils — stand on a coil pad until it lights.
  • Wardens shut coils off. Keep them away.
  • The Tree heals you. The Painter trades high-fives for upgrades.
  • Abilities — tap the bottom bar (keys 1–6).
